# 🤝 Diplomatic Negotiation Agent — Quick User Guide

*Get your negotiation game plan in about 1 minute. No setup, no jargon.*

---

## What is this?

A web app that turns any negotiation scenario into a **short, 1–2 page game plan**:
the 4–5 points to negotiate, your red lines, and the final agreement points you
should push for. Written in very simple English — anyone can understand it.

---

## Getting in 🔑

1. Open the app link you were given.
2. If it asks for an **Access PIN**, enter the PIN and press Enter.
3. That's it. *(The owner has already added the API key — you don't need one.)*

> Have your own Gemini key? You can paste it in the sidebar under
> *"Apni key use karni hai?"* — but you don't have to.

---

## Tab 1 — ⚡ Negotiation Points from a Brief

Use this when you receive a scenario (e.g. in a competition round).

1. Click the **⚡ Negotiation Points from a Brief** tab.
2. **Paste the brief** into the big box — *2–3 lines or 1–2 pages, both work.*
   - Short brief? The app expands it smartly (and tells you what it assumed).
   - Long brief? The app picks out only what matters.
3. **Fill the small boxes (optional):**
   - *Your stakeholder/role* — which side you represent (e.g. "India" or "Ministry of Water")
   - *Other parties* — who you're negotiating with (e.g. "Pakistan")
   - *Round* and *Time limit*
4. Press **⚡ Get my negotiation points (1-2 pages)**.
5. Wait ~1 minute → your document appears.

**What you get:**
- The situation in 3 lines
- Both sides at a glance + your red lines
- **A table of the 4–5 negotiation points** (your open / their open / landing zone)
- **The 4–5 final agreement points** — the deal to steer the room toward
- An opening line and a closing line you can say at the table

---

## Tab 2 — 🎲 Practice Generator

Use this to rehearse with your teammate.

1. Click the **🎲 Practice Generator** tab.
2. Pick a **category** (water dispute, security, crisis, global summit) and a
   **difficulty level**.
3. Press **💡 Get 2-3 practice topics** → you get 3 short ideas (2–3 lines each).
4. Choose one and press **📄 Make the 1-2 page negotiation document**.
5. Print it, split the roles with your teammate, start the timer.

## Quick Tips 💡

- **Judges check critical thinking first** — that's why every deal point in your
  document ends with a "→ why this works" line. Use those reasons out loud.
- **Venue announces a format for the agreement?** (pages / word count / clause style)
  — type it into the *"Agreement format announced at venue"* box before generating.
- **Round day:** the moment you get your brief, paste it in — don't wait.
- **Trust but verify:** the app is smart, but say a fact at the table only if it
  sounds right. When in doubt, paraphrase: *"reports suggest…"*
- **Error "quota/quota exhausted"?** Wait 1 minute and press the button again,
  or paste your own free Gemini key in the sidebar.
- **Nothing happens after a click?** Check your internet, then retry.
- Documents are written in simple English on purpose — great for a fast read
  before walking to the table.
